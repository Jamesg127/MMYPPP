Why All Americans Are Lazy (Even You)
Delivery Services: Because delivery services are so much more efficient than humans, and because people don't want to cook, or go to the store, people are getting more lazy. By not going to the store or cooking from home, people are fulfilling their happy side, but you also need to fulfill your pain side, because you won't get better if you don't try something new. No pain no gain. It seems like its the most efficient way to do something, which it might be, but you won't benefit from it. Doing things like going to the store and biking to places instead of ordering can save you physical and mental health.
Labor-Saving Devices: Even things like dishwashers and washing machines that you think you couldn't live without, people before us thrived without those same things for thousands of years. We don't realize how much we rely on things - especially things that run on electricity - in our life. The microwave, you might use everyday, but that really just lets you cook food faster than if it were on the stove. Manually washing dishes and clothes is a lot more time consuming than it is now. Even things like robot vacuums are are a lot more time consuming than you think.
Redefined Leisure: Instead of people exploring the ravine behind their house like your grandpa said was his entertainment, people are playing video games. People like to play videogames and do other inactive stuff, because why work more and be more active, to have fun, when you can just sit in a couch or chair and talk with friends. There are even small things that contribute such as things like vacuums, that make cleaning the floor easier, to even robot vacuums that do it for us. Most of these things are things that we do every day, but may not realize how much time it saves us.
Artificial Intelligence: A massive contributive to laziness recently has been AI. Why should I have to work for 8 hours a day when all I have to do is ask AI to do it for me? AI has made it so much easier for people to use their job, and when you can be lazy AND still get paid for doing nothing, that's a significant bonus. But AI has made it so much more difficult for newer generations to comprehend things that we should know because AI is basically giving us our information, without telling us how it got that information, which makes us lose knowledge and the process of understanding. We can sometimes rely on AI so much that we forget the foundational skills that everyone should know, an example being spelling. Foundational skills are essential because every single complex skill is built on top of them. Take swimming, for example: why buy the most expensive gear or grind through intense workouts just because someone tells you to, without understanding why you are doing it? If you start from the basics and build your way up naturally, you discover the purpose behind each technique. You learn how to get better through genuine understanding, rather than just blindly logging more laps than everyone else every day.
Conclusion: In Conclusion, people want to be lazy because that's easier. But having an easy life doesn't mean you have a good life. A good life is where you have everything you need and want and can do fun things, but a lazy life is just living on the edge, doing things you need to survive, but never preparing for your future. This is like the instant gratification mindset, where if you spend all of your money now instead of saving it for later, you might be happier now, but you will lose out on being happier later. Because you don't know what is going to happen later, you should be less lazy and be more active and do what will be good for you in the long run.
Entertainment Streaming: The phenomenon known as the Zeigarnik Effect is one of the reasons that streaming services are making us lazy. The Zeigarnik effect is when you remember unfinished tasks better than finished tasks, leading to people finishing things that they remember, which are the things that they like more; which may be things that are less valuable than more important things.
Instant Gratification Mindset: The instant gratification mindest is when you do something that you will immediately like, such as playing videogames with you friends, watching TV, hanging out with you friends, and in general, being lazy. But the opposite of this is when you will be gratified later on, such as going to the gym everyday, going on a run, studying for a test, and being active in general. People want to have that instant gratification because it makes them feel good, whereas the longer gratification mindset makes you feel good later, but people don't want to wait for that.
Working From Home: When Covid-19 hit, people started working from home a lot more, 12% of people in the US started working from home, increasing their laziness by a lot. When you don't have somewhere to be, you will be more lazy. Most jobs that you don't work from home are also very active, like waiting a restaraunt, or way back when, when you had to whack a piece of iron or steel for a couple of hours, or when you had to lay thousands of bricks per day.
Burnout: Burnout just makes you want to be lazy and not be productive It's difficult to do things that yo don't want to do at that current time, even if it means you will benefit in the future, a common example being work. People that are burnt out usually are burnt out from their work or school, and don't want to do those things because they are tired and bored of doing those things, even though they need to do those things.
